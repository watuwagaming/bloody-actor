01
CONTEXT / BRIEF

East Africa's gaming scene had passionate players but no media brand built for them. No client and no external brief. I set the target myself: build a gaming community for the region, and the content engine to serve it.

02
THE_CHALLENGE

Start from absolute zero. No audience, no budget, no team. Earn credibility in a niche global gaming brands overlook, on platforms where the algorithm only rewards consistency and production quality I would have to deliver solo.

03
APPROACH / METHOD

I treated it like a product launch, not a hobby. I defined the brand and a navy-and-pink visual identity, then split the channels by job: YouTube for long-form and news, TikTok for short-form reach. Everything ran on one repeatable system, the Watu Wa Gaming News format, so output stayed weekly and on-brand.

04
DELIVERABLES

One person, the full stack of a media brand:

05
