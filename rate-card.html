How We Work
Booking. 50 percent deposit confirms the slot, balance due on delivery.

Launch rates. Brand launch bundles are a market entry rate for the first three months, then standard rates apply.
Batching. Bundle pricing assumes products arrive together and are shot in one session. Separate shoots are quoted separately.
Revisions. Two rounds included on every deliverable.
